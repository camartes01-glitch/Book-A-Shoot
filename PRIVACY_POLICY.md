1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Book A Shoot, a photography and creative-services booking platform operated by GURRAM SAI AMARNATH, under the business/platform Camartes Photography Ecosystem ("Book A Shoot", "we", "us", "our" or "Platform").

Book A Shoot enables customers to discover, compare, request, communicate with and book photography and related creative services, including photographers, videographers and other event-production services.

Depending on the services available in your location, the Platform may facilitate bookings for services such as:

- Photography
- Videography
- Traditional photography
- Candid photography
- Traditional videography
- Candid videography
- Drone services
- LED wall services
- Web Live / live-streaming services
- Album design
- Video editing and post-production
- Other photography, videography and event-production services introduced on the Platform

Book A Shoot may allow customers to select event dates, event times, event locations, event types, service requirements, budgets and other booking preferences, and may facilitate communication and negotiations between customers and service providers.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, protect and otherwise process personal information when you:

1. visit or use www.bookashoot.online;
2. use the Book A Shoot Android application;
3. use the Book A Shoot iOS application;
4. create or maintain a Book A Shoot account;
5. search for or request photography or related services;
6. communicate with service providers or customers;
7. make or receive bookings;
8. make or receive payments through supported payment methods;
9. contact our support team; or
10. otherwise interact with Book A Shoot.

By using Book A Shoot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Where applicable, we obtain consent or rely on another lawful basis permitted by applicable law for processing personal information.
